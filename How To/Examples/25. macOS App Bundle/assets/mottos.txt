the dial sleeps; only the stars keep score
an hour that belongs to no one
the deepest part of the night watch
the tide of sleep turns
first grey in the east
the birds start their accounting
the sun clears the wall
the shadow is long and honest
the day has been let out of its stall
work weather; the shadow shortens
the morning burns off its excuses
the gnomon stands nearly alone
noon, and nothing is hidden
the shadow leans east and lengthens
the long afternoon of small tasks
the light turns the colour of brass
the shadow reaches for the garden wall
the low sun flatters everything
supper light; the dial gives up
the face goes to shade
lamps beat the sky
the last honest hour
the dial keeps time out of habit
the night takes the reckoning back
